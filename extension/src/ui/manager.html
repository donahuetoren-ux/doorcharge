    Supported formats
    • Your Blooket or Kahoot spreadsheet. If you built the game from Blooket’s or Kahoot’s import template, copy the cells (including the header row) from Google Sheets or Excel and paste them, or save the sheet as CSV. Any sheet with a header row works: a question column, answer columns and a “Correct answer” column holding the answer number(s) like 2 or 1,3, the letter, or the answer text.
    • One question per line:
      What is the capital of France? | Paris
How many legs does a spider have? | 8
    • Q:/A: lines:
      Q: What is 7 × 8?
A: 56
    • JSON: [{"question": "…", "answer": "…"}], or questions with "answers": [...] and "correctAnswers": [...], or choices marked "correct": true.

    A question with several accepted answers can be listed on several lines.

    Your own quiz pages

    Blooket and Kahoot work automatically. To use Quiz Autopilot on a quiz page you made, open that page, click the extension icon and choose “Enable on this site”.
